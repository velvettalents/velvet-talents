This website uses cookies to improve its service.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your computer or mobile device through your web browser when you visit it. Cookies help the website recognise your device the next time you visit.

In this policy, we use the term cookies to refer to all files that collect information in this way. Some cookies are essential to ensure the functionality provided, while others are intended to improve performance and the user experience. If you do not agree to the use of cookies, you should not continue to use our website after the cookie notice is displayed.

2. Accepting/refusing cookies

You may choose at any time to accept or refuse the installation of cookies on your device by configuring your browser software.

2.1 If you accept cookies

Whether a cookie is stored on your device is up to you. You may express and change your choice at any time, free of charge, using the options provided by your browser software.

If you have allowed cookies to be stored on your device in the browser software you use, the cookies embedded in the pages and content you have viewed may be stored temporarily in a dedicated area of your device. There, they can be read only by the party that issued them.

2.2 Why accept cookies?

When you visit our website www.velvet-talents.com, these cookies record certain information stored on your device. This information is used, in particular, to suggest products based on items you selected during previous visits, allowing you to enjoy a better browsing experience on our site.

2.3 If you refuse cookies

If you refuse cookies, we will nevertheless install a “refusal” cookie. This cookie allows us to remember your choice so that we do not ask you on every visit whether you wish to accept or refuse cookies.

2.4 Configuring your browser

How cookies are managed depends on each browser and is explained in your browser's help menu. In current browsers, these options are usually found in the privacy and security section of the settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies;
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data;
  • Safari: Settings (or Preferences) > Privacy;
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions.

Depending on the browser, these settings allow you to:

  • Delete cookies;
  • Block cookies by default;
  • Allow cookies by default;
  • Accept only cookies from the site you are visiting, refusing third-party cookies;
  • Create exceptions for cookies from specific websites or domains.
